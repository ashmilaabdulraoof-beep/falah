Common questions

Prayer times look a few minutes off

Open Settings in the app and choose the calculation method your local masjid uses, and the Asr madhab (Shafi'i or Hanafi).

Reminders don't arrive

Check that notifications are allowed for Zainab in your phone's settings. On Android, also allow "Alarms & reminders" and turn off battery optimisation for Zainab.

Where is my data?

Only on your phone. See the privacy policy.
