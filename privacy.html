Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 November 2026

Zainab: Daily Deeds ("Zainab", formerly Falah; "the app") is built so that nothing about you leaves your phone. This policy explains what the app uses and why.

We do not collect your data

Zainab has no account, no sign-in, no servers, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. The app makes no network requests with your information. We, the developers, cannot see anything you do in the app.

What stays on your phone

  • Location. If you allow it, Zainab reads your location to calculate prayer times. The calculation happens on the phone. The name of your town is also looked up on the phone, from a built-in list of places (GeoNames, CC BY 4.0); Zainab does not use any online map or geocoding service. Zainab itself never sends your location anywhere and stores it only on the phone. To find your position, your phone's location service may briefly use the network (on Android, Google Play services sends nearby Wi-Fi and mobile network information to Google to work out an approximate location). That is part of your phone's operating system and is used only at that moment, to show prayer times. You can refuse or revoke location access at any time in your phone's settings.
  • Your progress. Deeds you mark as done, skipped or missed, your prayer log, counter totals and evening check-in answers are saved in a local database on the phone.
  • Family. Names and optional phone numbers you type into the Intentions screen (for example "Mother"), and when you last called them, are stored only on the phone. Tapping the call button hands the number to your phone's dialler. Zainab does not read your contacts or call log.
  • Settings. Your name (if you enter one), language, calculation method and reminder choices are stored on the phone.

Deleting the app deletes all of this data. Your phone's own backups (iCloud or Google backup) may include app data according to your phone's settings. That is handled by Apple or Google, not by us.

Notifications

Reminders are local notifications scheduled on your phone. They are not sent through any server, and no push token is created.

Children

Zainab does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above. If a future version of Zainab ever needs to send data anywhere, we will say so here and in the app before it happens.
