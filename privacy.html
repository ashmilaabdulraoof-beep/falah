Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 November 2026

Falah: Daily Deeds ("Falah", "the app") is built so that nothing about you leaves your phone. This policy explains what the app uses and why.

We do not collect your data

Falah has no account, no sign-in, no servers, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. The app makes no network requests with your information. We, the developers, cannot see anything you do in the app.

What stays on your phone

  • Location. If you allow it, Falah reads your location to calculate prayer times. The calculation happens on the phone. Your location is stored only on the phone and is never sent anywhere. You can refuse or revoke location access at any time in your phone's settings.
  • Your progress. Deeds you mark as done, skipped or missed, your prayer log, counter totals and evening check-in answers are saved in a local database on the phone.
  • Family. Names and optional phone numbers you type into the Intentions screen (for example "Mother"), and when you last called them, are stored only on the phone. Tapping the call button hands the number to your phone's dialler. Falah does not read your contacts or call log.
  • Settings. Your name (if you enter one), language, calculation method and reminder choices are stored on the phone.

Deleting the app deletes all of this data. Your phone's own backups (iCloud or Google backup) may include app data according to your phone's settings. That is handled by Apple or Google, not by us.

Notifications

Reminders are local notifications scheduled on your phone. They are not sent through any server, and no push token is created.

Children

Falah does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above. If a future version of Falah ever needs to send data anywhere, we will say so here and in the app before it happens.
